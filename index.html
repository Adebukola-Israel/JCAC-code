Welcome to Jesus Christ Anointed Church — A Place to Experience God’s Love and Power



At Jesus Christ Anointed Church, we are a family of believers who love God, love people, and seek to share the life-changing power of Jesus Christ with everyone we meet.


Whether you’re joining us for the first time or looking for a church to call home, we invite you to come as you are and experience God’s presence. Together, we grow in faith, encourage one another, and serve our community — all for the glory of God.
